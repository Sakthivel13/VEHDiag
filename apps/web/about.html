About

Independent engineering, professional diagnostics

VEHDiag exists to prove a point: a modern, professional vehicle-diagnostics platform can be designed, engineered and shipped from first principles — with its own brand, its own architecture and its own tests.

Mission

Give mechanics, workshop owners, fleet operators and vehicle owners a diagnostics stack that is transparent, extensible and honest about what it does. The reference analysis that shaped the product is published in the repository under research/ — every observation labeled OBSERVED, INFERRED or NOT PUBLICLY OBSERVABLE.

What VEHDiag is not

VEHDiag is not a clone. It is not a renamed copy of any other product. It shares a class of capability — OBD-II diagnostics, live data, DTCs, VIN, freeze frames, EV architecture, reports — and implements that class with independent code, an independent purple design system, an independent protocol stack and an independent simulator.

Independent by construction

  • Protocols from the specifications — OBD-II modes, ISO-TP and UDS are implemented against public standards (SAE J1979, ISO 15765-2/4, ISO 14229).
  • Simulator-first development — virtual ECUs speak real ELM327 AT commands, so behavior is testable without vehicles.
  • Everything testable — unit, integration and end-to-end tests run in CI; nothing ships untested.
  • Own assets — logo, icons, illustrations and copy are original VEHDiag work.
