How it works

Three steps. Any vehicle.

FAQ

Common questions

Does VEHDiag work with my OBD adapter?

VEHDiag speaks ELM327 AT/OBD commands over Bluetooth, USB, TCP/WiFi, and CAN via an abstraction layer. Any standard ELM327 v1.4+ adapter works; the diagnostic engine is never hard-wired to one vendor.

Does it support electric vehicles?

Yes — the architecture is EV-first. Pluggable ECU profiles cover BMS, VCU, motor controller, OBC, DC-DC, charger, inverter and telematics, with EV live-data signals (SOC, SOH, cell voltage, DC bus voltage/current, motor temperature).

What if I don't have a vehicle handy?

The built-in VEHDiag Simulator emulates ECUs, CAN traffic, DTCs, freeze frames, VIN and live signals behind a real ELM327 AT-command terminal — you can develop and demo the whole platform without hardware.

Which protocols are implemented?

OBD-II modes 01/02/03/04/07/09/0A, UDS (0x10, 0x11, 0x14, 0x19, 0x22, 0x27, 0x2E, 0x31, 0x34, 0x36, 0x37, 0x3E), ISO-TP (SF/FF/CF/FC with STmin, block size, timeouts) and a CAN hardware-abstraction layer (SocketCAN, PCAN, Kvaser, Vector, Simulation).

Is my data private?

VEHDiag stores your vehicles, sessions and reports under your account with role-based access. Passwords are hashed with scrypt; nothing is shared. Self-hosted deployment is fully supported (see docs).

Is there a subscription?

The core platform is free. Pro and Workshop plans add multi-user teams, advanced reporting and priority support — see the pricing page.
